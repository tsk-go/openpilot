# "Galaxy: StarPilot Companion" Privacy Policy

Google Play listing: **Galaxy: StarPilot Companion**  
Last updated: October 25, 2026

**Galaxy: StarPilot Companion** is the Android companion app for [StarPilot](https://github.com/firestar5683/StarPilot), open-source driving software based on openpilot. The app appears as **Galaxy** on your phone and displays the Galaxy interface served by StarPilot.

## Data used

- **Location:** If you enable phone GPS sharing, Galaxy sends your location, accuracy, time, and available altitude, speed, and direction to StarPilot for navigation. Sharing can continue in the background with a notification. You can turn it off in Galaxy or revoke location permission in Android settings.
- **Bluetooth:** Galaxy uses nearby-device access to pair and connect to StarPilot. It stores Bluetooth addresses and an encrypted pairing key on your phone, and sends your phone's name and pairing information to StarPilot.
- **App data:** Galaxy stores connection settings and phone-internet usage totals on your phone. It loads the Galaxy interface from StarPilot over local HTTP and saves a copy for Bluetooth use.
- **Galaxy controls and shared content:** Galaxy sends your settings and navigation requests to StarPilot. If you share text from another app through Android's Share menu, Galaxy sends it to StarPilot for navigation.
- **Network data:** Galaxy and StarPilot can use Wi-Fi or your phone's cellular data, depending on the connection and features in use. Mobile data usage and carrier charges may apply. Your carrier and services used through StarPilot, such as maps or cloud services, may process network traffic under their own privacy policies.

## Storage and deletion

You can remove Galaxy's saved pairing in the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data. This does not remove data already held by StarPilot or other services.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the dedicated [StarPilot support ticket process](https://wiki.firestar.link/ticketsystem/) and ask for the Android app support. Alternatively, you may contact using the developer contact email address listed on Google Play Store page.
